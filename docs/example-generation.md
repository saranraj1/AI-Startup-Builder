# Example generation

Input: `I want to build an AI platform that helps college students prepare for interviews.`

Forgeway returns a score, market sizing assumptions, competitors, a business model canvas, pricing tiers, brand system, landing copy, MVP surfaces, technical architecture, AI evaluation plan, legal checklist, investor narrative, marketing launch plan, and documentation outline.

Numbers such as TAM, MRR, CAC, and break-even are explicitly labeled as estimates. They are hypotheses for validation, not investment or legal advice.
